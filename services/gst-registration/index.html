THE SERVICE IN DETAIL

A clear purpose.
A considered approach.

This service helps you organize your business information and move through the agreed preparation and submission steps for a GST registration application. The focus is on clear communication, accurate information supplied by you and visibility into the application stage.

Start by explaining your business and whether you already have a registration or an application in progress. New registration, account-access problems, cancellation issues and ongoing filing are different needs; the appropriate scope should be identified before work begins.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clear starting point.

Is this an official government service?

No. This is independent application assistance. There is no government affiliation or authority to approve an application.

Can you guarantee approval or a 24-hour certificate?

No. Approval, additional-information requests and processing time depend on the relevant authority and the application.

I already have a GST registration. Should I request this service?

Tell me the existing status and the problem you need help with first. Access recovery, cancellation matters, changes and filing are not automatically covered by a new-registration assistance scope.

Which documents should I send?

Begin with your business description and existing application status. A case-specific checklist is discussed after review; do not send identity documents or passwords through the general appointment form.

Does registration assistance include return filing?

Ongoing filing is not automatically included. Any filing support, recurring work or separate advice must be agreed independently.
