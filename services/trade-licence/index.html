THE SERVICE IN DETAIL

A clear purpose.
A considered approach.

Trade licence assistance starts with your business activity and the location where it operates. The application process must be considered in that context, so the first step is understanding your situation and identifying the relevant application pathway.

I help coordinate information, prepare the agreed application materials and communicate follow-up steps. This is an independent assistance service; it does not replace the authority’s review or guarantee an approval date.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clear starting point.

Can you provide a fixed document list for every business?

The checklist needs to be reviewed for the business activity, location and application type. Start with those details so the appropriate information can be identified.

Is the authority’s fee included in your quote?

The proposal should clearly distinguish the assistance fee from any authority charges. Confirm the breakdown before proceeding.

Can you guarantee approval?

No. Review, requests for additional information, approval and processing time are controlled by the relevant authority.

Does the service include renewal?

Renewal or changes to an existing licence are not automatically included in new-application assistance. Describe the task so it can be scoped correctly.

Can I send my documents through the appointment form?

Use the form to explain your requirement and provide contact details. Sensitive documents should be shared only through an agreed channel after the scope is discussed.
