THE SERVICE IN DETAIL

A clear purpose.
A considered approach.

A good storefront helps customers understand your products, explore the catalog and move confidently toward a purchase. I plan the Shopify experience around your brand, product range and the information shoppers need before ordering.

The project can cover initial store setup, a redesign or improvements to an existing store. We agree the catalog size, collections, pages and integrations first, then review the shopping journey on mobile as well as desktop.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clear starting point.

How many products will you upload?

The proposal specifies the number of products and how variants are counted. Send your catalog so the required entry and organization work can be estimated.

Can you connect payments and delivery services?

Setup assistance can be included for supported providers. Account verification, provider approval, service availability and fees remain separate from the development work.

Can you redesign an existing Shopify store?

Yes. Share the current store and the changes you need. We can review navigation, product pages, branding and the mobile experience before agreeing the scope.

Are marketplace setup and registration assistance included?

They are separate services unless your written proposal explicitly includes a combined offer. Confirm the exact deliverables and any exclusions before proceeding.

Will the store guarantee sales?

No. Store development creates the shopping experience, while results also depend on products, pricing, traffic, marketing and fulfilment. Marketing support can be discussed separately.
