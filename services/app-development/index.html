GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clear starting point.

Can one project support Android and iOS?

A cross-platform approach can be considered. We review device-specific requirements and the intended experience before choosing the implementation.

Do I need an admin panel?

If you need to manage products, bookings, users or content, an administration interface may be useful. Its features and permissions are scoped separately.

Can you add payments or notifications?

These features can be included after reviewing the selected providers and requirements. Account approval, messaging permissions and provider charges may apply.

Is app-store approval guaranteed?

No. Submission and review are controlled by the relevant platform. Release preparation can be included, but approval and timing cannot be guaranteed.

Can the app connect to my existing website?

Possibly, if the existing system offers suitable APIs or can be extended. Share the current setup so the integration work can be assessed.
