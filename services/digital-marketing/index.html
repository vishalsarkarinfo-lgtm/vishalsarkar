A clear purpose.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clear starting point.

Should I choose Meta Ads or Google Ads?

We discuss your offer, customer journey and available budget before choosing a channel. The right starting point depends on what you sell and how people discover or search for it.

Is advertising spend included in your fee?

Not unless the proposal explicitly says so. The management fee and platform advertising budget should be clearly separated.

Will you guarantee leads or sales?

No. Outcomes depend on the offer, budget, competition, creative, website and follow-up, among other factors. Campaign work focuses on testing and improving the agreed setup.

Can you help improve lead quality?

We can review messaging, qualification questions, targeting and the inquiry journey. Your feedback on actual conversations is important when evaluating quality.

What reports will I receive?

Reporting frequency and metrics are agreed in the scope. Useful measures may include spend, inquiries and available conversion data, together with an explanation of what the numbers mean.
