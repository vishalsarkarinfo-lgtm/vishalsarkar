THE SERVICE IN DETAIL

A clear purpose.
A considered approach.

Customer information is difficult to use when it is spread across chats, notebooks and spreadsheets. A custom CRM brings the important details, activity history and next follow-up into one organized workspace.

I begin with your existing process: how a lead arrives, who responds, which stages it moves through and what information your team needs. The aim is a practical system people can use consistently, with features introduced in manageable phases.

WHAT THE WORK CAN INCLUDE

Beyond a feature list.

Your proposal confirms the exact deliverables. These areas help us shape the right scope.

01

Lead & customer records

Agree the fields, stages and activity history your team needs. Keep records organized around real business decisions rather than unnecessary data collection.

02

Follow-ups & task ownership

Plan task assignment, due dates and follow-up views so each team member can see what needs attention. Reminder channels are included only when specifically scoped.

03

Roles & working views

Define who can see, create, change or export information. Plan practical dashboards for the owner, managers and employees as required.

04

Reports & data handover

Create agreed reports and discuss imports, exports and backup responsibilities. Existing data is reviewed before migration is quoted.

GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions.
A clear starting point.

Can my existing Excel data be imported?

Potentially. Share an anonymized sample so the fields, duplicate records and data quality can be assessed. Cleaning and migration work need a separate agreed scope.

Can each employee have a separate login?

Yes, individual accounts and role-based access can be included. We define the permissions before development and test the agreed restrictions.

Can it connect to my website?

A website inquiry form or other supported integration can be scoped. Access to the relevant service and its integration capabilities must be checked first.

Will it send WhatsApp or email reminders automatically?

Only if that integration is explicitly included. Provider setup, message permissions, templates and recurring charges are separate considerations.

Can more modules be added later?

Yes. We can plan a phased system and review new requirements after the initial workflow is in use. Additional modules are quoted separately.
