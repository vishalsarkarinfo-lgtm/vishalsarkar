HOW TO GET STARTED

A useful brief.
A clearer proposal.

Tell me about your business, the problem you want to solve and the result you want the service to support. Include your existing website or tools, the features you consider essential and your preferred budget and timeframe.

We then agree the scope, deliverables, responsibilities, fees and review points. Third-party subscriptions, platform charges, advertising spend and ongoing support are clarified where relevant. You can start with one service or request a coordinated scope across several.

BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Common questions.

Can I combine more than one service?

Yes. A website, CRM or marketing project can be discussed together. Each workstream, dependency and deliverable is listed in the proposal so the combined scope remains clear.

Do you work with businesses outside West Bengal?

Yes. I am based in Cooch Behar, West Bengal, and available for remote projects across India. Any location-specific assistance is reviewed before acceptance.

Do you offer a fixed price for every project?

Pricing depends on the work involved. Share your requirements for a tailored quote that explains the deliverables, third-party costs and any ongoing fees.

Will I work directly with you?

Yes. The service is presented under my professional brand, Vishal Sarkar, and requirements and project communication are handled directly with me.

How do I request a quote?

Use the appointment form or WhatsApp link to share your business, the service you need and your requirements. An appointment request is confirmed separately after I contact you.
