Custom website or WordPress?

Choose around your workflow, content and future changes.

Vishal Sarkar · Practical planning guide

Start with the people who update it

WordPress can suit teams that want a familiar content editor. Custom development can suit a more specific product experience or business workflow. Both approaches still need thoughtful design and maintenance.

Define the moving parts

List content types, forms, user roles and integrations before choosing a stack. A custom interface does not automatically require a custom backend.

Think beyond launch

Discuss backups, updates, support and who owns the source, accounts and content. The right choice is the one your business can operate comfortably.
