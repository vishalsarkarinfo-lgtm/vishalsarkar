How CRM can help a small business

Bring follow-ups and customer information into view.

Vishal Sarkar · Practical planning guide

Make the next step visible

A follow-up list can help you see which customer needs a reply and what was discussed last time. Ownership and dates give each task context.

Reduce repeated searching

A shared customer record can replace information scattered across notes and spreadsheets. Decide what belongs in the CRM and how sensitive data should be protected.

Review the work

Simple reports can show pipeline stages and unfinished tasks. Choose reports that support an actual weekly decision.
